Tolled bridge

Humber Bridge

The Humber Bridge carries the A15 across the Humber estuary between Hessle in the East Riding of Yorkshire and Barton-upon-Humber in North Lincolnshire.

Essential information

How the Humber Bridge toll works

The Humber Bridge uses Automatic Number Plate Recognition cameras to record vehicle registration numbers as vehicles cross the bridge.

Drivers can pay as a guest or use a prepaid Humber Bridge Toll Account. Account holders receive discounted toll prices, but must ensure that the correct vehicle registration is attached to the account and that sufficient credit is available.

The payment screen may not show your journey

When paying as a guest after travelling, the system does not necessarily display the crossing after you enter your vehicle registration. You must purchase enough Guest Crossings to cover the journeys you made.

Current charges

Humber Bridge toll prices

The operator currently publishes the following standard and account-holder charges:

Vehicle type Without an account With an account
Motorcycles and trikes Free Free
Two-axle vehicles under 3.5 tonnes, including cars, small vans and caravans £2.00 £1.50
Two-axle vehicles between 3.5 and 7 tonnes, minibuses, buses, coaches and agricultural vehicles £5.00 £4.00
Vehicles with three or more axles over 7.5 tonnes £15.00 £12.00

Vehicle classification can depend on weight, number of axles and vehicle type. Check the operator’s current classification before travelling.

Ways to pay

Paying the toll

Guest Crossing

A Guest Crossing can be purchased without opening a prepaid account. You can purchase crossings in advance or by midnight on the day after travelling.

Guest Crossings can be purchased online, through the official app, at a PayPoint location or by telephone.

Humber Bridge Toll Account

A prepaid account provides discounted toll prices. ANPR cameras match the vehicle registration to the account and the toll is deducted from its balance.

Account holders should check their vehicle registration, account balance and automatic top-up arrangements.

Do not wait for a journey to appear

The official guidance says that Guest Crossing users should buy enough crossings to cover their travel. Payment must be completed within the payment window even if the particular journey is not displayed.

Payment deadline

When payment must be made

If you do not have an account, payment must be completed by midnight on the day after you cross.

The same deadline applies if you have an account but there was insufficient credit, or if the correct vehicle registration was not attached to the account.

Example

If you cross on Monday, payment must be completed by midnight on Tuesday. The deadline applies seven days a week.

A payment made after the deadline may be allocated to a future crossing rather than the journey for which an Unpaid Toll Notification has already been triggered.

Unpaid tolls

Unpaid Toll Notifications

The operator says that an Unpaid Toll Notification, or UTN, will be triggered if the toll has not been paid by the deadline. A £25 administration fee is then added to the outstanding toll.

A UTN may be issued when:

  • The toll was not paid within the payment window
  • An account did not contain sufficient credit
  • The vehicle registration was missing from the account or entered incorrectly

If you receive a UTN

  1. Check the vehicle registration and crossing date.
  2. Preserve receipts, emails, PayPoint slips, screenshots and account statements.
  3. Check whether the vehicle was correctly registered on your account or concession.
  4. Use the official UTN portal to pay or submit an appeal.
  5. Keep copies of everything you submit.
Appeal before paying

The operator states that a UTN can only be appealed if it has not already been paid and has not been passed to its debt-recovery agency. Check the current instructions on the notice before taking action.

Concessions

Disability concessions

The Humber Bridge provides free crossings for people who meet its disability-concession requirements. A Blue Badge by itself does not qualify a person for free crossings.

Applicants must open a Humber Bridge Toll Account, add their vehicle and apply for the concession. Approval is required before relying on free travel.

The published eligibility requirements include receipt of the qualifying mobility component of Disability Living Allowance or Personal Independence Payment, together with an eligible tax-exempt vehicle.

Registration and renewal fees

The operator currently states that new applicants pay a one-off £10 concession registration fee, followed by a £5 annual renewal fee. Verify these amounts before applying.

Evidence

What records should you keep?

  • The correct vehicle registration number
  • Payment confirmation emails and receipt IDs
  • PayPoint receipts
  • Account statements and balance records
  • Automatic top-up confirmation
  • Screenshots of payment or account errors
  • Concession approval and renewal emails
  • Copies of notices, appeals and operator responses
  • The envelope in which a notice arrived

A bank transaction can help demonstrate that money was taken, but retain the operator’s receipt or payment reference as well because it may show which registration and crossing the payment was intended to cover.
