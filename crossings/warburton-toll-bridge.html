Bridge profile

Warburton Toll Bridge

Independent information about crossing charges, payment methods, exemptions and Unpaid Toll Charge Notices for the Rixton and Warburton Toll Bridge.

Visit the official bridge website Manage a toll notice

Information last checked: 28 August 2026

Standard toll £1 per passage

For eligible cars, vans and buses.

Daily cap £2 per vehicle

Maximum charge within one calendar day.

Published deadline Midnight the following day

According to the official operator’s website.

Payment system ANPR

Vehicle registrations are recorded by cameras.

Official payment information

Paying the toll

The bridge operator states that payment must be made by midnight on the day after crossing.

Payment can be made through:

  • The official website using guest payment
  • A prepaid account
  • The official mobile application
  • The automated telephone line
  • Crossing tokens purchased in advance
Keep your confirmation

The official website says a successful payment produces a confirmation screen and reference number. A confirmation email should also be issued.

Discounts and exemptions

Who may qualify?

Blue Badge holders

Vehicles carrying an eligible Blue Badge holder may qualify for an exemption, but an account and approved application are required. Displaying a Blue Badge alone does not automatically register the vehicle.

Local residents

A 50% discount is available to eligible registered keepers whose main residence is within:

  • WA3 6 — Risley
  • WA13 9 — Lymm
  • M31 4 — Partington

Other exempt vehicles

Exemptions may also apply to motorcycles, mopeds, emergency service vehicles on duty, eligible Crown and military vehicles, breakdown-recovery vehicles and qualifying local public-service buses.

Unpaid toll notices

Unpaid Toll Charge Notice amounts

The bridge’s confirmed byelaws specify the following unpaid toll charges in addition to the original toll:

Stage Unpaid toll charge Total including £1 toll
Initial level £30 £31
Second level £60 £61
Later level £100 £101

Additional administration, debt-recovery or legal costs may subsequently be claimed. The basis and recoverability of any additional amount may depend on the individual circumstances.

Do not ignore formal correspondence

Check whether the document is from Excel Parking Services, DCBL, DCB Legal or the County Court. These organisations have different roles and powers.

Wording requiring care

Official deadline and byelaw wording

Official operator

Published payment instruction

The operator’s website and terms state that payment must be made by midnight on the day after crossing.

Confirmed byelaws

“Relevant date” wording

The byelaws define the relevant date as the second day after the crossing and refer to the toll remaining unpaid after that date.

Possible interpretation—not a guaranteed outcome

There appears to be a difference between the operator’s published deadline and the wording used in the confirmed byelaws. This website does not claim that a particular interpretation has been conclusively decided by a court.

Motorists should follow the earlier deadline published by the operator wherever possible. Anyone relying on the byelaw wording in an existing dispute should consider obtaining individual legal advice.

Protecting your position

Evidence to retain

  • Crossing date and approximate time
  • Vehicle registration entered during payment
  • Payment reference and confirmation email
  • Relevant bank or card entry
  • Account activity and available balance
  • Approved exemption or discount confirmation
  • Screenshots of website or payment errors
  • Original notices, letters and envelopes
  • Copies of appeals and responses
