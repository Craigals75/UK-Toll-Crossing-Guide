Official payment information

Paying the toll

Payment must be made in advance or by midnight on the day after travelling through the Tyne Tunnels.

Payment options

  • Online guest payment through the TT2 website
  • TT2 prepaid account
  • TT2 mobile application
  • Automated telephone line on 0191 574 0030
  • Cash payment through a PayPoint retailer
  • Up to 10 passages purchased in advance
Advance passages

Up to 10 passages may be purchased in advance and remain valid for up to 12 months.

Late payments

TT2 states that a payment made after the deadline will be allocated to a future passage rather than the journey already made.

Disability exemptions

Blue Badge and disability-tax accounts

Blue Badge holders and people with an eligible disability-tax-class vehicle may apply for free travel through the tunnels.

  • An exemption account must be approved in advance
  • Only one vehicle can normally be registered
  • The registered vehicle is recognised through ANPR
  • Exemptions normally last for a maximum of 12 months
  • The exemption must be renewed annually
  • The registered vehicle can normally be changed once per month
Displaying a Blue Badge is not sufficient

TT2 no longer provides inspection lanes for unregistered Blue Badge users. The vehicle must be attached to an approved exemption account before travelling.

For a Blue Badge exemption, the badge holder must normally be present in the vehicle. If the exempt user is not present, the toll must be paid.

Unpaid Toll Charge Notices

If the toll is not paid

TT2 states that a UTCN is automatically issued when the toll has not been paid by midnight on the day after travel.

Approximate stage UTCN amount Original toll
Day 1 £30 Also payable
Day 15 £60 Also payable
Day 30 £100 Also payable
Collection-agency stage £100 plus up to £79 Also payable

TT2 describes the dates as illustrative because notices can be processed in batches and bank holidays may affect the timetable.

Wait for the UTCN before appealing

TT2 states that an appeal cannot be submitted until the UTCN has been issued. If you want to pay before the letter arrives, contact TT2 directly.

Appeals process

Appealing a Tyne Tunnels UTCN

Tyne Tunnels uses its own internal UTCN appeal process. This is different from the statutory Traffic Penalty Tribunal process used by Dart Charge and Merseyflow.

  • TT2 states that each case may be appealed up to three times
  • The charge should be placed on hold while an appeal is assessed
  • TT2 aims to respond within 14 days
  • Supporting evidence can be uploaded with the appeal
  • Postal appeals are also accepted
  • Paying the UTCN normally closes the case and ends the appeal
Debt-collection stage

If the case has already been transferred to a third-party collection agency, TT2 says the appeal must be made through that agency’s escalation process.

Protecting your position

Evidence to retain

  • Journey date, direction and approximate time
  • Vehicle registration and vehicle class
  • Payment reference and receipt
  • Prepaid-account balance and activity
  • PayPoint receipt and reference number
  • Exemption approval and expiry date
  • Screenshots of payment or account errors
  • The UTCN, reminders and original envelopes
  • Copies of every appeal and response
