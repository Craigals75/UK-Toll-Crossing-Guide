How payment works

Paying for the M6 Toll

The M6 Toll differs from Dart Charge, Merseyflow and the Tyne Tunnels. Drivers normally pay as part of their journey through a toll plaza or use a registered Breeze account.

Toll prices depend on the vehicle class and how many of the motorway’s charging zones are used.

Payment options

  • Contactless debit or credit card at the toll plaza
  • Breeze account using automatic number-plate recognition
  • Mobility Exemption Pass for approved users
  • Deferred Payment Notice where authorised at the toll plaza
Do not drive away without resolving payment

If you cannot pay at the toll plaza, use the assistance button and follow the operator’s instructions. A Payment Notice may be issued so the journey can be paid afterwards.

Disability exemption

Mobility Exemption Pass

Eligible disabled people may apply for a Mobility Exemption Pass allowing free travel on the M6 Toll when the approved vehicle and named person are travelling together.

  • A £7 application fee applies
  • The pass is normally valid for one year
  • A new application is required annually
  • The application can take up to seven working days
  • Journeys before approval remain chargeable
  • The named eligible person must be present in the vehicle

Examples of qualifying evidence

  • V5C showing the tax class as Disabled
  • Qualifying Motability documentation
  • Enhanced-rate PIP mobility component
  • Higher-rate DLA mobility component
  • Armed Forces Independence Payment
  • War Pensioners’ Mobility Supplement
A Blue Badge alone does not establish eligibility

The M6 Toll states that the Blue Badge scheme does not distinguish between people who qualify for this exemption and those who do not. The required benefit or vehicle-tax evidence must be provided.

Deferred Payment Notices

If you receive a Payment Notice

A Payment Notice may be issued when a journey cannot be paid for normally at the toll plaza. It allows a limited period for the outstanding journey charge to be settled.

Action Published deadline Possible consequence
Pay the Payment Notice Within six days of validation Journey charge settled
Appeal the Payment Notice Within six days of validation Evidence will be required
Leave the notice unpaid After the six-day period At least £70 additional charges may be added

The notice may take up to 48 hours to become available online while the journey images and route are checked.

Contact the operator if the notice cannot be found

If the Payment Notice is still unavailable after 48 hours, contact M6 Toll Customer Services rather than allowing the six-day deadline to expire.

Appealing a Payment Notice

Internal appeal process

The M6 Toll states that an appeal must be made within six days following the date the journey was validated.

  • Contact Customer Services on 0330 660 0790
  • Explain why the notice is disputed
  • Provide supporting evidence
  • Keep a written record of the appeal
  • Record the date, time and name of anyone spoken to

This is not the same statutory Tribunal process used for Dart Charge or Merseyflow PCNs.

Protecting your position

Evidence to retain

  • Journey date, time, entry and exit junctions
  • Vehicle registration and vehicle class
  • Toll-plaza payment receipt
  • Breeze account statement and balance
  • Payment Notice number and validation date
  • Mobility Exemption Pass approval
  • Copies of correspondence and appeal evidence
  • Details of telephone conversations
