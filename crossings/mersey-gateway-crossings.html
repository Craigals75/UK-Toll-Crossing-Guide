Official payment information

Paying for a crossing

A charge applies for each one-way journey across either the Mersey Gateway Bridge or Silver Jubilee Bridge.

Payment can be made up to 12 months in advance or by 11:59pm on the day after crossing.

Payment options

  • Online through the official Merseyflow website
  • Through the Merseyflow mobile application
  • By telephone on 01928 878 878
  • At the Merseyflow walk-in centre
  • At a participating Payzone outlet
  • Through a registered Merseyflow account
Each direction is a separate crossing

A return journey normally requires payment for two crossings unless an approved discount, exemption or unlimited travel plan applies.

Accounts and discounts

Available plans

Pre-pay account

Registered users may receive a discount on individual crossings. Sticker-registered vehicles receive a larger discount than video-registered vehicles.

Monthly travel passes

  • Unlimited Class 2 travel: £110 per month
  • Class 2 off-peak travel: £75 per month

Halton residents

Eligible Halton residents using a qualifying Class 2 vehicle can apply for the Local User Discount Scheme. The standard annual administration fee is £12, with a reduced renewal fee potentially available through automatic renewal.

Disability exemption

Blue Badge holder plan

A Blue Badge holder can register one vehicle for unlimited crossings at no additional crossing charge after paying the one-off £5 registration fee.

  • The exemption must be approved before it can be used
  • Only one vehicle may be registered
  • The Blue Badge holder must be present in the vehicle
  • The registered vehicle does not have to belong to the badge holder
  • Both sides of the Blue Badge must be supplied when applying
Motability vehicles are not automatically exempt

A Motability vehicle still requires payment unless it has been successfully registered under an eligible Blue Badge holder plan or another applicable scheme.

Penalty Charge Notices

If the toll is not paid on time

A statutory PCN may be issued to the registered keeper when a crossing has not been paid for by the deadline.

PCN stage Penalty Original toll
Paid within 14 days £25 Also payable
Standard PCN £50 Also payable
Charge Certificate £75 Also payable

The £50 PCN is reduced to £25 if paid within 14 days. If it remains unpaid and is not challenged within the applicable period, a £75 Charge Certificate may be issued.

Do not make a late crossing payment without checking

Merseyflow states that a toll payment made after the deadline may not automatically be allocated to the previous crossing. If a PCN has been issued, follow the instructions on that notice.

Challenging a PCN

Representations and Tribunal appeals

1

Make representations

Challenge the PCN using the instructions provided by Merseyflow. Representations should normally be made within 28 days.

2

Receive the decision

Halton Borough Council, through Merseyflow, will decide whether the representation is accepted or rejected.

3

Independent appeal

A Notice of Rejection should explain how to appeal to the independent Traffic Penalty Tribunal.

Traffic Penalty Tribunal

The Tribunal cannot normally consider an appeal until representations have first been made to the charging authority and a Notice of Rejection has been issued.

Protecting your position

Evidence to retain

  • Bridge used, direction, date and approximate time
  • Vehicle registration and vehicle class
  • Payment confirmation and receipt
  • Merseyflow account activity and balance
  • Blue Badge or local resident plan approval
  • Screenshots of payment errors
  • The PCN and original envelope
  • Copies of representations and appeal correspondence
