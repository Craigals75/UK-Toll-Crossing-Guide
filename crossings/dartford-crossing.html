Dartford Crossing

Independent information about Dart Charge payments, vehicle charges, disability exemptions and Penalty Charge Notices for the Dartford Crossing between Kent and Essex.

For a one-off or pay-as-you-go Class B crossing.

Pre-pay car charge £2.80

For eligible Class B vehicles using a pre-pay account.

Payment deadline Midnight the following day

Charging hours 6am to 10pm

Charges apply every day, including bank holidays.

Official payment information

Paying the Dart Charge

The Dart Charge normally applies when the crossing is used between 6am and 10pm. It is free to cross between 10pm and 6am.

Payment must be made in advance or by midnight on the day after crossing.

Payment options

  • One-off payment without creating an account
  • Pay-as-you-go account with an attached payment card
  • Pre-pay account with discounted crossing charges
  • Cash payment at participating Payzone stores
  • Payment through the Dart Charge contact centre
  • Advance payment by post
Advance payments

A one-off payment made before travelling remains available for 12 months and should be used automatically for the next eligible crossing by that vehicle.

Disability and local concessions

Exemptions and discounts

Disability exemption

You do not need to pay if the vehicle qualifies for free UK vehicle tax because of a disability. The vehicle should be checked automatically when it crosses.

A Blue Badge by itself is not the test

The Dart Charge exemption is linked to the vehicle’s disability tax status. Holding or displaying a Blue Badge does not automatically make every vehicle exempt.

Local resident discount

Eligible residents within the council boundaries of Dartford or Thurrock can apply for a local resident plan.

  • £25 per year for unlimited crossings
  • £12.50 per year for 50 crossings
  • £6.25 for 25 additional crossings on the limited plan

Eligibility documents must be renewed periodically and the vehicle must remain correctly registered to the account.

Penalty Charge Notices

If the Dart Charge is not paid

A statutory Penalty Charge Notice may be sent to the registered keeper if the crossing charge is not paid within the required time.

PCN stage Penalty Original crossing charge
Paid within 14 days £35 Also payable
Standard penalty £70 Also payable
Increased penalty £105 Also payable

The £70 penalty is normally reduced to £35 when paid within 14 days. It may increase to £105 if it remains unpaid.

Do not ignore a PCN

Dart Charge uses a statutory civil-enforcement process. This differs from an ordinary private debt-recovery demand and can progress to a Charge Certificate, Order for Recovery and enforcement agents.

Challenging a PCN

Representations and independent appeal

1

Make representations

Challenge the PCN directly with Dart Charge using the instructions and deadline printed on the notice.

2

Receive the decision

If the challenge is rejected, Dart Charge should issue a Notice of Rejection explaining the next step.

3

Appeal to the Tribunal

Following a Notice of Rejection, an appeal may be made to the independent Traffic Penalty Tribunal.

Traffic Penalty Tribunal

You cannot normally appeal directly to the Tribunal before first making representations to Dart Charge. After a Notice of Rejection, the usual appeal period is 28 days.

There is no fee for submitting an appeal, but the full PCN will normally be payable if the appeal is unsuccessful.

View the official Dart Charge appeal process

Later enforcement

Charge Certificates and Orders for Recovery

If a PCN progresses without being paid or challenged, the process may include:

  1. Penalty Charge Notice
  2. Charge Certificate
  3. Debt registration
  4. Order for Recovery
  5. Warrant and civil enforcement agents
Different procedures apply at this stage

If you did not receive the original PCN, made representations but did not receive a response, or appealed without receiving a decision, an Order for Recovery may include a statutory declaration procedure. Follow the deadline printed on the document.

Protecting your position

Evidence to retain

  • Crossing date, direction and approximate time
  • Vehicle registration and vehicle class
  • Payment confirmation and transaction reference
  • Dart Charge account statements
  • Bank or payment-card evidence
  • Disability vehicle-tax status
  • Local resident discount approval
  • Copies of the PCN and every subsequent notice
  • Representations, evidence and appeal decisions
  • Original envelopes showing postal dates
