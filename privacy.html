Your information

Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information may be collected when you visit or contact UK Toll Crossing Guide and how that information is handled.

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Website operator

Who is responsible for your information?

UK Toll Crossing Guide is an independently operated information website.

For questions about this privacy policy or personal information submitted directly to the website owner, contact:

Privacy contact

Email: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]

This website is not operated by a toll operator, government authority, tribunal, debt-recovery company or enforcement organisation.

Current website functions

Information collected directly by this website

The current version of UK Toll Crossing Guide does not require visitors to create an account, sign in or provide personal information simply to read its pages.

The website does not currently operate:

  • User accounts
  • Online toll payments
  • An appeal-submission service
  • Advertising
  • Email newsletters
  • Behavioural profiling
  • Independent website analytics
  • A contact or evidence-upload form
This policy must be updated if the website changes

If analytics, advertising, donations, mailing lists, contact forms or document uploads are introduced, this policy and any cookie information must be reviewed before those services are activated.

Hosting

Information processed by GitHub Pages

This website is currently hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub.

GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages website is visited, the visitor’s Internet Protocol address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub.

GitHub may also process technical information needed to provide, secure and maintain its hosting service.

This processing is controlled by GitHub and is subject to GitHub’s own privacy documentation.

Read GitHub’s Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab)

Contacting the website

Information you send by email

If you contact the website owner by email, the following information may be received:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The contents of your message
  • Documents or photographs you choose to attach
  • Technical information contained in the email

This information will normally be used to:

  • Read and respond to your enquiry
  • Investigate a requested correction
  • Check reports about toll information
  • Prevent abuse, fraud or misuse of the website
  • Keep a record of important editorial decisions
Do not send unnecessary personal documents

Do not send unredacted bank statements, payment cards, driving licences, passports, benefit letters, Blue Badges, court forms or medical records unless specifically necessary and requested.

Remove unrelated personal information, reference numbers, addresses, barcodes and QR codes before sending evidence.

Legal basis

Why information may be processed

Where UK data-protection law applies, personal information submitted directly to the website owner will normally be processed because it is necessary for the legitimate interests of:

  • Responding to enquiries
  • Maintaining accurate website information
  • Keeping the website secure
  • Establishing or responding to legal claims
  • Preventing misuse of the service

Information may also be processed where necessary to comply with a legal obligation.

If consent is required for a particular activity, it will be requested separately and may be withdrawn.

Sensitive information

Health and disability information

A person contacting the website may choose to mention a disability, medical condition or qualifying benefit when reporting a problem involving a toll concession.

This type of information can receive additional protection under data-protection law. Visitors should provide only the minimum information necessary to explain their enquiry.

The website does not require medical evidence to provide general information about a toll operator’s published concession scheme.

Public submissions

Reports and personal experiences

Information sent privately will not automatically be published.

If information from a personal experience may help explain a wider issue, permission should be obtained before publishing information that identifies the contributor.

Wherever practical, published reports will be anonymised and unnecessary personal information will be removed.

Do not submit another person’s private information unless you have a lawful and appropriate reason to do so.

Sharing information

Who may receive personal information?

Personal information submitted directly to the website will not be sold.

It may be disclosed only where reasonably necessary:

  • To service providers used for email or website hosting
  • To investigate a security incident or misuse
  • To obtain professional advice
  • To comply with a legal obligation or court order
  • To establish, exercise or defend legal rights
  • Where the person concerned has agreed to the disclosure

Contacting this website does not submit an appeal to a toll operator. Information will not routinely be forwarded to an operator on your behalf.

Data retention

How long information is kept

Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received.

Routine enquiries that no longer need action should normally be deleted when they are no longer required. Information may be retained for longer where necessary to:

  • Maintain a record of an important correction
  • Respond to an ongoing complaint or dispute
  • Meet a legal obligation
  • Protect or defend legal rights
  • Investigate fraud, abuse or a security incident

The appropriate retention period will depend on the nature of the information and the reason it was supplied.

Security

Protecting personal information

Reasonable steps will be taken to protect personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access or disclosure.

However, no method of sending information over the internet or by email can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Cookies

Does this website use cookies?

The current website does not intentionally set its own advertising, analytics or personalisation cookies.

The mobile navigation script only opens and closes the menu. It does not store information in the visitor’s browser.

The hosting provider or an external website may operate its own cookies or similar technologies. Their use is governed by the relevant provider’s privacy and cookie information.

External links

When you follow a link to a toll operator, GOV.UK, a tribunal or another external service, that organisation may collect information and set cookies under its own policies.

External websites

Third-party services

UK Toll Crossing Guide contains links to websites operated by third parties. The website owner does not control how those organisations collect or use personal information.

Visitors should read the privacy information provided by the external service before submitting payment, vehicle, account, appeal or personal information.

Your rights

Data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including the right to:

  • Ask whether their personal information is held
  • Request access to their personal information
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected
  • Request deletion in certain circumstances
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office

These rights are not absolute and may depend on the reason the information is being processed.

Complaints

Information Commissioner’s Office

If you are concerned about how your personal information has been handled, you should first contact the website owner so the concern can be investigated.

You also have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office, which is the UK regulator for data protection.
