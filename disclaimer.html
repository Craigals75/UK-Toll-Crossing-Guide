About this website

Independent information service

UK Toll Crossing Guide is an independently operated information website about toll roads, bridges, tunnels, payment arrangements, concessions and toll notices in the United Kingdom.

This website is not affiliated with, approved by, sponsored by or operated on behalf of any toll operator, government department, local authority, tribunal, enforcement company, debt-recovery company or court.

This is not an official payment website

Payments, account registrations, appeals and formal representations must be made through the relevant operator or authority using its official website or contact details.

Nature of the information

General information only

Information published on this website is provided for general guidance. It is intended to help visitors identify the relevant toll crossing, understand published operator information and locate official payment or appeal services.

The information does not take account of every person’s individual circumstances and should not be treated as a substitute for checking the current requirements with the relevant toll operator or authority.

Professional advice

Not legal, financial or professional advice

Nothing published on UK Toll Crossing Guide constitutes legal, financial, debt or other professional advice.

Appeal templates and example wording are provided as general drafting assistance. They do not guarantee that an appeal, representation, complaint or court defence will succeed.

Visitors are responsible for ensuring that any statement they submit is truthful, complete and appropriate to their own circumstances.

Formal proceedings

Consider obtaining independent advice if you have received a Letter of Claim, county court claim, Order for Recovery, enforcement notice or other formal legal document.

Accuracy

Prices, deadlines and procedures can change

Reasonable efforts are made to use official sources and show when crossing information was last checked. However, toll prices, payment deadlines, exemptions, terms, website addresses and enforcement procedures can change without notice.

UK Toll Crossing Guide does not guarantee that every page will always be complete, current or free from error.

Before travelling, paying or appealing, visitors should verify the current information directly with the relevant toll operator or authority.

Deadlines

Responsibility for payment and appeal deadlines

Visitors remain responsible for identifying and meeting any applicable payment, appeal, representation or court deadline.

Using this website, contacting its owner or submitting a correction does not suspend, extend or replace an official deadline.

Do not wait for a response from this website

If a deadline is approaching, contact the relevant operator, authority, tribunal or court using the details provided on the official notice.

Official services

Payments and appeals

UK Toll Crossing Guide does not process toll payments, manage toll accounts, issue exemptions or decide appeals.

Following a link from this website does not mean that a payment, application or appeal has been completed. Visitors should check for an official confirmation screen, email or reference number from the relevant operator.

Visitors should retain receipts, confirmation numbers and copies of anything submitted through an external website.

Links to other organisations

External websites are controlled by their respective owners. UK Toll Crossing Guide is not responsible for their availability, content, security, accessibility, privacy practices or any changes they make.

Visitors should check that an external website is genuine before entering payment, account or personal information.

Community reports will be distinguished from confirmed operator information, legislation, court decisions and independently verified evidence.

Publication of a report does not mean that UK Toll Crossing Guide accepts every allegation or conclusion contained in it as established fact.
