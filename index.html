Notice and payment help

Have you received a toll notice or recovery letter?

Do not ignore formal correspondence. First identify which organisation issued it and what stage the case has reached.

1

Identify the document

Check whether it is a toll notice, penalty notice, debt-recovery letter, Letter of Claim or court claim.

2

Preserve your evidence

Keep payment references, confirmation emails, account activity, exemption approvals, screenshots and envelopes.

3

Check the correct process

Appeal and enforcement arrangements vary between toll operators and cannot be treated as one national system.

Important

Never ignore a Letter of Claim, tribunal document or county court claim. These documents have strict response deadlines.

Accessible travel

Disability concessions and exemptions

Some UK toll crossings provide free or discounted travel for disabled people or vehicles carrying a Blue Badge holder.

Eligibility and registration rules differ. Many crossings require an application to be approved before travel.

Check government concession information (opens in a new tab)

Before travelling, check:

  • Whether advance registration is required
  • Which vehicles can be registered
  • When the exemption becomes active
  • Whether the concession must be renewed
  • What evidence you should retain

About this project

Clear information without pretending uncertain issues are settled

UK Toll Crossing Guide aims to separate official rules, legal wording, possible interpretations and reports submitted by members of the public.

Information will be linked to its source and include the date it was last checked. Community reports will be clearly labelled and will not be presented as proven facts without supporting evidence.
